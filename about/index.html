About Ricobene's

A Chicago late-night staple since 1946.

Since 1946

Ricobene's opened its doors in Chicago in 1946 and became a staple for late-night eating in the city. Generations of regulars have come in for the breaded steak sandwich, the pizza and the Italian beef, and plenty still come by after midnight.

We're at 252 W 26th St in Bridgeport, open until 12:30 AM most nights and 2 AM on Fridays and Saturdays.
